Common questions

How do I sync between my phone and computer?

Open Settings, then Continue with Google under Account & cloud sync, on each device. They sync each time you open the app and when you come back to it.

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, everything stays on your device. An account only adds sync, plus a copy of your history that survives losing your phone.

How do I back up my history?

Settings → Backup downloads a file of everything you've logged. Restore, in the same place, loads a backup file back in.

Why don't I see a set I logged on another device?

Open the app on the device that has the set, so it can sync, then reopen it on the other. If a device logged workouts while signed out, signing in combines them with your synced history rather than replacing either.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account & cloud sync → Delete account. This permanently erases your account and everything synced to it. Download a Backup first if you want to keep your history. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

What happens to my data?

See the privacy policy. In short: no ads, no tracking, and your data is used only to keep your devices in sync.
